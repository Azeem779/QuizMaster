🪨 GEOLOGICAL STRUCTURE OF INDIA – NOTES
🟢 1️⃣ Geological Evolution of India

India ka geological structure plate tectonics aur geological history ka result hai.

Major stages:

Precambrian Shield formation

Gondwana sedimentation

Deccan volcanism

Himalayan mountain building

🪨 2️⃣ Peninsular Shield (Precambrian Block)

India ka sabse purana geological unit hai.

Features:

Very stable landmass

Hard crystalline rocks

Igneous & metamorphic rocks

Regions:

Aravalli range

Dharwar rocks

Bundelkhand plateau

⚠ Age:
Precambrian (>540 million years)

🌍 3️⃣ Gondwana Formation

Time:
👉 Paleozoic–Mesozoic era

Origin:
India was part of Gondwana supercontinent.

Includes:

India

Africa

Australia

Antarctica

South America

⛏ 4️⃣ Gondwana Rocks Importance

Gondwana sediments contain:

Coal deposits

Sandstone

Shale

Major coal fields:

Damodar Valley

Mahanadi basin

🌋 5️⃣ Deccan Trap Formation

Period:
👉 Late Cretaceous

Cause:
👉 Massive volcanic eruptions

Characteristics:

Basalt rocks

Layered lava flows

Black soil formation later

Area covered:

Maharashtra

MP

Gujarat

🌊 6️⃣ Tethys Sea

Ancient ocean between:

Indian plate

Eurasian plate

Sediments deposited here later formed Himalayan rocks.

🏔 7️⃣ Himalayan Orogeny

Cause:
👉 Collision of Indian plate with Eurasian plate

Result:

Fold mountains

Tectonic uplift

Ongoing mountain building

🌾 8️⃣ Indo-Gangetic Foredeep

When Himalaya uplifted:

A foreland basin formed

Filled with sediments from rivers

Result:

👉 Northern Plains of India

🧠 9️⃣ Major Geological Divisions of India

Peninsular Shield

Himalayan Region

Indo-Gangetic Plains

⚠ PGT Exam Traps

Peninsular plateau = oldest landmass

Himalaya = youngest mountain system

Deccan traps = volcanic origin

Gondwana rocks = coal bearing
