⚡ ENERGY RESOURCES OF INDIA – NOTES
🟢 1️⃣ Meaning of Energy Resources

Energy resources =
Wo resources jinse power generate ki ja sakti hai.

Example:

Coal

Petroleum

Hydropower

Solar energy

🌍 2️⃣ Classification of Energy Resources

Energy resources 2 types ke hote hain:

1️⃣ Conventional Energy

Traditional energy sources.

Examples:

Coal

Petroleum

Natural Gas

Hydroelectric power

2️⃣ Non-Conventional Energy

Renewable energy sources.

Examples:

Solar energy

Wind energy

Tidal energy

Nuclear energy

Biomass energy

🪨 3️⃣ Coal

India ka largest energy source.

Major coalfields:

Jharia

Raniganj

Bokaro

Talcher

Major states:

Jharkhand

Odisha

Chhattisgarh

West Bengal

Use:

Thermal power plants

Steel industry

🛢 4️⃣ Petroleum

Main energy fuel for transport.

Major oil fields:

Mumbai High

Digboi

Ankleshwar

Krishna-Godavari Basin

Uses:

Fuel

Petrochemicals

🌊 5️⃣ Hydroelectric Power

Electricity from flowing water.

Major projects:

Bhakra Nangal

Hirakud

Tehri Dam

Advantages:

Renewable

Clean energy

☀ 6️⃣ Solar Energy

Energy from sun radiation.

Major solar parks:

Bhadla Solar Park (Rajasthan)

Pavagada Solar Park (Karnataka)

India ka aim:

👉 National Solar Mission

🌬 7️⃣ Wind Energy

Wind turbines se electricity.

Major states:

Tamil Nadu

Gujarat

Maharashtra

Karnataka

India top wind energy producers me se ek hai.

☢ 8️⃣ Nuclear Energy

Energy from nuclear fission.

Major nuclear plants:

Tarapur

Kalpakkam

Kudankulam

Narora

Fuel:

👉 Uranium
