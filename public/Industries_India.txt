🏭 INDUSTRIES OF INDIA – NOTES
🟢 1️⃣ Meaning of Industry

Industry =
कच्चे माल को manufactured goods में बदलने की प्रक्रिया।

Example:

Steel industry

Textile industry

Sugar industry

🌍 2️⃣ Classification of Industries
1️⃣ Agro-Based Industries

Raw material agriculture से मिलता है।

Examples:

Cotton textile

Sugar industry

Jute industry

2️⃣ Mineral-Based Industries

Raw material minerals से मिलता है।

Examples:

Iron & Steel

Aluminium

Cement

3️⃣ Small Scale Industries

Small investment

Local raw materials

Labour intensive

Examples:

Handloom

Cottage industries

4️⃣ Large Scale Industries

Large capital

Advanced technology

Mass production

Examples:

Automobile industry

Steel industry

🔩 3️⃣ Iron & Steel Industry

Iron and steel = basic industry
क्योंकि यह अन्य industries को raw material देता है।

Major steel plants:

Bhilai (Chhattisgarh)

Bokaro (Jharkhand)

Durgapur (West Bengal)

Rourkela (Odisha)

🧵 4️⃣ Cotton Textile Industry

India की oldest modern industry।

Major centers:

Mumbai

Ahmedabad

Surat

Coimbatore

Reasons for location:

Raw cotton

Humid climate

Labour availability

🌾 5️⃣ Sugar Industry

Raw material:

👉 Sugarcane

Major states:

Uttar Pradesh

Maharashtra

Karnataka

🪢 6️⃣ Jute Industry

Raw material:

👉 Jute fiber

Major region:

👉 Hugli belt (West Bengal)

Reason:

Raw material supply

Labour availability

💻 7️⃣ IT Industry

India का fastest growing sector।

Major IT cities:

Chennai
