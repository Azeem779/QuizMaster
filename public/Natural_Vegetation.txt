🌳 NATURAL VEGETATION OF INDIA – NOTES
🟢 1️⃣ Meaning of Natural Vegetation

Natural vegetation =
Wo plants jo natural conditions (climate, soil, rainfall) me bina human interference ke grow karte hain.

Example:

Forests

Grasslands

Mangroves

🌍 2️⃣ Factors Affecting Natural Vegetation
1️⃣ Climate

Main factors:

Temperature

Rainfall

Humidity

Example:

High rainfall → dense forests

2️⃣ Soil

Different soil → different vegetation

Example:

Alluvial soil → deciduous forests

3️⃣ Relief

Altitude change → vegetation change

Example:

Himalaya → alpine vegetation

🌲 3️⃣ Types of Natural Vegetation in India

Champion & Seth classification ke according 5 main types:

1️⃣ Tropical Evergreen Forests
2️⃣ Tropical Deciduous Forests
3️⃣ Tropical Thorn Forests
4️⃣ Mountain Forests
5️⃣ Mangrove Forests

🌳 4️⃣ Tropical Evergreen Forests

Rainfall:

👉 More than 200 cm

Features:

Dense forests

Tall trees

Evergreen leaves

Regions:

Western Ghats

Andaman Nicobar

North-East India

Examples:

Ebony

Rosewood

Rubber

🌾 5️⃣ Tropical Deciduous Forests

Rainfall:

👉 100–200 cm

Features:

Trees shed leaves in dry season

Regions:

Central India

Northern plains

Types:

Moist deciduous

Dry deciduous

Examples:

Teak

Sal

🌵 6️⃣ Tropical Thorn Forests

Rainfall:

👉 Less than 50 cm

Regions:

Rajasthan

Gujarat

Deccan plateau

Examples:

Acacia

Cactus

🌲 7️⃣ Mountain Forests

Found in:

👉 Himalaya

Vegetation changes with altitude:

Temperate forests

Alpine forests

Examples:

Pine

Deodar

🌊 8️⃣ Mangrove Forests

Found in:

👉 Delta regions

Conditions:

Saline water

Tidal influence

Example:

Sundarbans

Important tree:

👉 Sundari tree

⚠ PGT Exam Traps

Mangrove forests = tidal regions

Teak = deciduous forests

Sundarbans = largest mangrove forest

Evergreen forests = highest rainfall
