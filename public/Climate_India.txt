🌦 CLIMATE OF INDIA – NOTES
🟢 1️⃣ Nature of Indian Climate

India ki climate ko generally Tropical Monsoon Climate kaha jata hai.

Main features:

Seasonal reversal of winds

Monsoon rainfall

High temperature variation

🌍 2️⃣ Factors Affecting Climate of India
1️⃣ Latitude

India ka major part Tropic of Cancer ke south me hai
Isliye climate tropical nature ka hai.

2️⃣ Altitude

Higher altitude → Lower temperature

Example:

Himalaya region cold

Plains warmer

3️⃣ Distance from Sea

Coastal areas:

Moderate climate

Interior regions:

Extreme climate

Example:

Mumbai → moderate

Delhi → continental

4️⃣ Himalayan Barrier

Himalaya:

Cold winds ko block karta hai

Monsoon rainfall ko influence karta hai

🌧 3️⃣ Monsoon Mechanism

Indian monsoon ka main cause:

Land–sea temperature contrast

ITCZ shift

Jet streams

Tibetan plateau heating

🌦 4️⃣ Seasons of India

IMD ke according 4 seasons hote hain.

❄ 1️⃣ Cold Weather Season

Time:

January – February

Features:

Low temperature

Western disturbances rainfall

🔥 2️⃣ Hot Weather Season

Time:

March – May

Features:

High temperature

Dust storms

Loo winds

🌧 3️⃣ Southwest Monsoon

Time:

June – September

Features:

Major rainfall

Arabian Sea branch

Bay of Bengal branch

🍂 4️⃣ Retreating Monsoon

Time:

October – December

Features:

Monsoon withdrawal

Cyclones in Bay of Bengal

Rain in Tamil Nadu

🌪 5️⃣ Western Disturbances

Origin:

Mediterranean region

Impact:

Winter rainfall in North India

Snowfall in Himalaya

🌊 6️⃣ Tropical Cyclones

Mainly occur in:

Bay of Bengal

Arabian Sea

More frequent in Bay of Bengal.

⚠ PGT Exam Traps

Tamil Nadu rain mainly NE monsoon se hoti hai

Bay of Bengal cyclones > Arabian Sea cyclones

Himalaya monsoon ko block karta hai
