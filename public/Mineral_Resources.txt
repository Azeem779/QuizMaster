⛏ MINERAL RESOURCES OF INDIA – NOTES
🟢 1️⃣ Meaning of Mineral Resources

Minerals =
Naturally occurring inorganic substances jo earth crust me milte hain aur economic value rakhte hain.

Example:

Iron ore

Coal

Bauxite

Copper

🌍 2️⃣ Classification of Minerals

India me minerals ko mainly 2 categories me divide kiya jata hai.

1️⃣ Metallic Minerals

Metals produce karte hain.

Examples:

Iron ore

Manganese

Copper

Bauxite

2️⃣ Non-Metallic Minerals

Metal produce nahi karte.

Examples:

Limestone

Mica

Gypsum

⛏ 3️⃣ Iron Ore

India ka most important mineral.

Major types:

Hematite

Magnetite

Major regions:

Odisha

Jharkhand

Chhattisgarh

Karnataka

Important belt:

👉 Odisha–Jharkhand belt

🪨 4️⃣ Coal

Coal = major energy mineral.

Types:

Anthracite

Bituminous

Lignite

Peat

Major coalfields:

Jharia

Raniganj

Bokaro

Talcher
