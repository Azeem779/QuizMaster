🌱 SOILS OF INDIA – NOTES
🟢 1️⃣ Meaning of Soil

Soil =
Weathered rock particles + organic matter + water + air

Soil formation process ko Pedogenesis kehte hain.

🌍 2️⃣ Factors of Soil Formation

5 main factors:

1️⃣ Parent rock
2️⃣ Climate
3️⃣ Relief
4️⃣ Organisms
5️⃣ Time

🌾 3️⃣ Major Soil Types in India

India me 8 major soil types milte hain:

1️⃣ Alluvial Soil
2️⃣ Black Soil
3️⃣ Red Soil
4️⃣ Laterite Soil
5️⃣ Desert Soil
6️⃣ Mountain Soil
7️⃣ Saline Soil
8️⃣ Peaty Soil

🌾 4️⃣ Alluvial Soil

Origin:

👉 River deposition

Regions:

Northern plains

Coastal plains

Types:

Bhangar (old alluvium)

Khadar (new alluvium)

Crops:

Wheat

Rice

Sugarcane

🌑 5️⃣ Black Soil (Regur)

Origin:

👉 Deccan Trap basalt rocks

Features:

High moisture retention

Cracks in dry season

Regions:

Maharashtra

Gujarat

MP

Crop:

👉 Cotton

🔴 6️⃣ Red Soil

Origin:

👉 Iron rich rocks

Features:

Low fertility

Needs fertilizers

Regions:

Tamil Nadu

Karnataka

Andhra Pradesh

🟤 7️⃣ Laterite Soil

Origin:

👉 Heavy rainfall + leaching

Regions:

Western Ghats

NE India

Crops:

Tea

Coffee

Rubber

🏜 8️⃣ Desert Soil

Regions:

Rajasthan

Gujarat

Features:

Sandy texture

Low moisture

🏔 9️⃣ Mountain Soil

Found in:

👉 Himalayan region

Types:

Forest soil

Alpine soil

⚠ PGT Exam Traps

Black soil = cotton soil

Bhangar = old alluvium

Khadar = new alluvium

Laterite = leaching process
