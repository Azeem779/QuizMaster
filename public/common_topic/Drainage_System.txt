🌊 DRAINAGE SYSTEM OF INDIA – NOTES
🟢 1️⃣ Meaning of Drainage System

Drainage System =
River aur uski tributaries ka network jo kisi area ka water drain karta hai.

Important terms:

Drainage basin

Watershed

River system

🌏 2️⃣ Major River Systems of India

India ke rivers 2 major groups me divide kiye jate hain:

1️⃣ Himalayan Rivers
2️⃣ Peninsular Rivers

🏔 3️⃣ Himalayan Rivers

Origin:
👉 Himalaya glaciers

Characteristics:

Perennial rivers

Large drainage basin

Meandering courses

Major systems:

Indus system

Ganga system

Brahmaputra system

🌊 4️⃣ Indus River System

Origin:
👉 Tibet (near Lake Mansarovar)

Major tributaries:

Jhelum

Chenab

Ravi

Beas

Sutlej

⚠ Mostly flows through Pakistan.

🌾 5️⃣ Ganga River System

Origin:
👉 Gangotri Glacier

Bhagirathi + Alaknanda
= Ganga

Major tributaries:

Left bank:

Ramganga

Gomti

Ghaghara

Gandak

Kosi

Right bank:

Yamuna

Son

🌊 6️⃣ Brahmaputra System

Origin:
👉 Tibet (Tsangpo River)

Features:

Deep gorges

Large sediment load

Braided channel in Assam

🪨 7️⃣ Peninsular Rivers

Characteristics:

Older rivers

Seasonal flow

Fixed courses

Major rivers:

Godavari

Krishna

Kaveri

Mahanadi

West flowing rivers:

Narmada

Tapi

🌊 8️⃣ Narmada & Tapi

Special rivers because:

Flow westward

Form estuaries instead of deltas

Reason:
👉 Rift valley structure

🧠 9️⃣ Drainage Patterns

Dendritic
👉 Tree-like pattern

Trellis
👉 Rectangular pattern

Radial
👉 Flow outward from central hill

🌾 🔟 Inland Drainage

Water does not reach sea.

Example:

Luni River

Rajasthan region

⚠ PGT Exam Traps

Brahmaputra = Tsangpo in Tibet

Narmada flows through rift valley

Himalayan rivers = perennial

Peninsular rivers = seasonal
