🇮🇳 PHYSIOGRAPHIC DIVISIONS OF INDIA – NOTES
🟢 1️⃣ Major Physiographic Divisions

India ko 6 major physiographic units me divide kiya jata hai:

Himalayan Mountains

Northern Plains

Peninsular Plateau

Indian Desert

Coastal Plains

Islands

🏔 2️⃣ Himalayan Mountains

Young Fold Mountains

Formation:
👉 Tethys Sea sediment + Plate collision

Three parallel ranges:

1️⃣ Himadri (Greater Himalaya)

Highest peaks

Permanent snow

2️⃣ Himachal (Lesser Himalaya)

Valleys and hill stations

3️⃣ Shiwalik

Outer Himalaya

Loose sediments

🌾 3️⃣ Northern Plains

Formation:
👉 Indus–Ganga–Brahmaputra river deposition

Features:

Alluvial soil

Fertile land

Flat topography

Subdivisions:

Bhabar

Terai

Bhangar

Khadar

🪨 4️⃣ Peninsular Plateau

Oldest landmass in India

Two parts:

1️⃣ Central Highlands
2️⃣ Deccan Plateau

Features:

Igneous & metamorphic rocks

Mineral rich

🏜 5️⃣ Indian Desert

Location:
👉 Western Rajasthan

Features:

Sand dunes

Low rainfall

Inland drainage

🌊 6️⃣ Coastal Plains

Two parts:

West Coast

Narrow

High rainfall

East Coast

Wide

Delta formation

🏝 7️⃣ Islands

Two groups:

1️⃣ Andaman & Nicobar

Volcanic origin

2️⃣ Lakshadweep

Coral islands

⚠ PGT Exam Traps

Himalaya = youngest mountain system

Peninsular plateau = oldest part

West coast narrow but rainfall high

East coast wide because delta
